# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router) + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS, with a lightweight motion library (Motion / `motion/react`). Specified directly by the user, not delegated.

## Users

Primary: Spanish homeowners (single-family houses, chalets) evaluating residential solar installation, comparing electricity-bill savings, and deciding whether to request a quote. Secondary: small/medium business owners evaluating commercial solar. Audience is design-conscious enough that the site itself (built by TwoSide Digital, a design agency) is also read as a portfolio proof-of-craft by prospective agency clients and hiring reviewers.

## Product Purpose

SOLARA is a fictional, conceptual solar-installation company built as a portfolio showcase for TwoSide Digital. The deliverable must read and function as a genuine top-tier commercial solar company website (Awwwards-caliber), not as a visibly-fake demo, while never explicitly claiming SOLARA is a real client or company. Success = a visitor believing this is a real premium solar installer's site, and a design reviewer believing this is agency-quality work.

## Positioning

Not a generic "green tech" installer. SOLARA's claimed mechanism: proprietary engineering + personalized energy study + certified installation + ongoing monitoring, sold through precision and daylight/energy craft rather than eco-guilt or corporate-green cliché. The site must avoid the sector's own template look (stock icon grids, generic green gradients).

## Operating Context

Single long-scroll marketing homepage (Persuade mode) for a premium photovoltaic installer, covering: hero (roof with visible panels), residential/business installations plus complements (batteries, monitoring, maintenance, EV chargers), panel product showcase with example configuration and installation components, interactive solar-flow diagram with day/night battery mode, solar installation calculator (panels, kWp, production, self-consumption, savings, payback), without/with panels energy comparison, 6-step installation process, conceptual installation cases, FAQ, solar-study lead form, footer. No corporate stats and no testimonials: SOLARA is fictional and must not claim track record.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All numeric content (calculator estimates, example configurations, conceptual installation cases) is fictional/demo data and must be visibly marked as estimates/demonstrations per the user's explicit brief, so the site never asserts real client results.
- No CMS/backend; content is static/local to the project.
- Must be built with real, high-quality solar/residential photography (sourced stock), not illustrations or fake div-based screenshots.
- Language: Spanish (all UI copy).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: SOLARA. Logo: wordmark-based (no pre-existing logo asset provided; a simple authored mark is acceptable).
- Base palette pinned by brief: warm cream/off-white + charcoal black.
- Accent pinned by user decision (2026-09-23, replacing the earlier amber): premium forest green as the single accent (#173F35 deep, #21604C primary, #2F7A5C interactive, #DDE9E2 / #EEF4F0 surfaces, sage #93C6AD for accents on carbon). Used strategically over cream + carbon; never neon, lime, saturated or eco-template green.
- Motion/intensity pinned by user decision (2026-09-22): "Premium contenido" - restrained, Apple/Linear-like subtlety over loud experimental choreography.
- Explicit dislikes (brief): template look, excessive cards, generic AI gradients, unnecessary icons, repetitive blocks, exaggerated shadows, distracting animation.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real company, no real testimonials, no real project data, no real photography of an actual SOLARA installation. All such content is authored as clearly-labeled demo/estimate material per the brief. Future work must not present any of it as real.

## Product Principles

1. Genre-fluent, not generic: read instantly as a serious solar-installation company, executed at a craft level the category rarely reaches.
2. One committed accent, used with intent (data highlights, CTAs, key moments), never scattered decoration.
3. Content over chrome: real photography and real numbers-shaped data carry the page; no card grids or icon walls as a structural crutch.
4. Every fictional claim (stats, testimonials, projects, calculator output) stays honestly labeled as estimate/demo without breaking the premium illusion of craft.
5. Motion is restrained and purposeful: it clarifies hierarchy and state, never performs for its own sake.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard navigable, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected, WCAG AA contrast minimum, semantic HTML, form fields properly labeled. No platform-specific requirement beyond standard web accessibility (explicitly requested by the user).
