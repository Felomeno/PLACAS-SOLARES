## Direction contract

THESIS: SOLARA proves premium solar through engineering precision and daylight itself, not eco-guilt or generic green-SaaS iconography. It refuses the sector's own default: stock leaf-icon grids, generic corporate green, and templated three-card feature rows.

OWN-WORLD: Warm cream (#FAF6EC) and warm carbon-black (#16140F) as the two base fields, alternated compositionally (cream for production/trust sections, carbon for data/close moments), with one committed solar-amber accent (#E3A64D) used as fill (buttons, bars, highlights) and, on dark ground only, as text. Bricolage Grotesque (display, large, confident) + Manrope (body). One radius scale: pill buttons, 20px photo panels, 10px controls. Full-bleed real photography carries the page; no icon-grid cards, no gradients, no kickers/eyebrows.

STORY: A Spanish homeowner lands, grasps "your roof could do more" in one viewport, plays with the savings calculator to see their own numbers, is walked through editorial (not card-grid) proof of solutions/process/projects, and converts through a short final form.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Asymmetric hero, no center-hero cliché: large left-aligned headline + subhead + dual CTA over a full-bleed golden-hour photo of a modern house, transparent nav overlaying the image, three inline micro-stats beneath the CTAs (typographic, not boxed cards).

FORM: brief-pinned direction, not rolled. The user's own detailed brief (palette family, mood words, explicit likes/dislikes, structure, Awwwards reference tier) plus a confirmed accent (amber over green) and confirmed restraint level (subtle, Apple/Linear-like over experimental) already fix every axis the concept-seed roll exists to discover; running it would relitigate decisions the user already made, not surface new ones.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
